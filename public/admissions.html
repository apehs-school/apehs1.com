Admissions Information

We welcome applications from students who are eager to learn and grow in a supportive, disciplined, and nurturing environment.

Enrollment Steps

  1. Immunization card (passport)
  2. Complete the application form
  3. Submit birth certificate and previous school records
  4. Attend student assessment and interview
  5. Receive acceptance letter

Please be advised that our Preparatory Department enrolls students aged 3 to 11 years. Our Extension High School offers placement for students in Grade 7 through Grade 11.
