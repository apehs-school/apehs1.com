OUR MISSION

Ardenne Preparatory and Extension High School seeks to provide quality education within a Christian environment where all stakeholders — the church, Board of Governors, staff, students, and parents — work together harmoniously to enable students to achieve their maximum potential. This is done within the ideals of community and in preparation for the next stage of their development.
